A free neon tilt maze game for Android. Tilt to roll, hit the targets, dodge the landmines, beat the clock.

Privacy Policy · 19 September 2026

TILT is made by Beaniedude. There are no accounts, no sign-in and nothing to type into, so the game never asks who you are.

What is collected

  • How the game is played: rounds started and ended, score, round length. Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Crash reports: the error, your device model and Android version. Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Your Advertising ID, used to show and count ads. Google AdMob.

What is never collected

  • Name, email or phone number. The game has no sign-in and no text input.
  • Location. TILT requests no location permission of any kind.
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Tilting the device is the control, so the accelerometer is read constantly. Those readings are used as they arrive, never stored and never sent anywhere.

Ads

One full-screen ad plays at the end of a round, never during one. There is nothing to buy in TILT, no subscription and no paid version. That single ad is the whole reason the game can exist, and it is the only thing that pays the developer for building it.

If you would rather not see it, turn your internet off and play. Nothing in the game needs a connection, so no ad loads, nothing is locked, no timer runs out and nothing is taken away. The game is the point. The ad is just how it got made.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked before any ad loads, and you can change that choice later. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. Google explains its side here.

Children

TILT is not aimed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Uninstalling stops all collection. To have collected analytics and crash data removed, email the address below and say so. It is deleted within 30 days.
